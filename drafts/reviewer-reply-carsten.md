1. Market — beyond Ukraine.

You're right to press on this. Ukraine is the acute catalyst, not the business
case. The chronic global drivers for critical long-bone defects are
well-established and larger than the current conflict by an order of magnitude:

• Infected non-unions and segmental defects — Lasanianos 2010; Calori 2014
• High-energy civilian trauma (road traffic, industrial, sports) — Giannoudis
  2006; Global Burden of Disease 2019
• Oncologic segmental resection — Mavrogenis 2012

Aggregate addressable population in the EU + US is in the 250,000–400,000
cases per year range, with the category sustained by aging demographics,
urbanisation, and oncology pipelines well beyond any conflict timeline.
The war is the origin story. The clinical case has to stand on global,
peacetime merit — and we believe it does.

2. EF without VAC vs. IF with VAC — does literature support VAC?

The literature supports VAC strongly for open-wound management (Armstrong &
Lavery 2005; DeFranzo 2001). But VAC accompanies external fixation because EF
leaves the wound open. With internal fixation, primary wound closure becomes
possible — which changes the equation. CITadel is not competing with VAC; it
removes the context in which VAC is necessary.

3. Why PMMA spacer, and not a stiff bone substitute that avoids the void?

This is the biology question, and it's the most important one. The PMMA spacer
is not a structural filler. It is a biological inducer. Within 4–6 weeks a
vascularised foreign-body membrane forms around it — the Masquelet induced
membrane (Masquelet 1986, 2000 series; Pelissier 2004; Aho 2013) — rich in
VEGF, TGF-β, and BMP-2. That membrane is what makes stage-2 grafting work.

Stiff bone substitutes close the void but do not replicate the induced-membrane
biology; they show poorer graft incorporation and higher infection retention
in contaminated beds (Giannoudis 2011 review). So CITadel deliberately
separates the roles:

• CITadel = mechanical / load-bearing element, internal stabilisation
• PMMA = biological scaffold for membrane induction

That separation is the core design choice, not an incidental compatibility.
